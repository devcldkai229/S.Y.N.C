## 3. Accuracy of the numbers

| Metric | Nature |
|---|---|
| BMR, TDEE | **Statistical estimate** (Mifflin-St Jeor); individual error can be ±10–20% |
| Calorie & macro targets | Estimates based on what you entered — **not a medical nutrition prescription** |
| Food calories and nutrients | From reference databases or supplied by Partners; **may differ substantially** from the actual dish |
| Calories burned in training | Rough estimate, not measured with medical devices |
| Recovery score, burnout risk, adherence | **Internal product signals** with no diagnostic value |

Do not use these numbers to make treatment decisions.

## 4. Consult a doctor before use if you

- are **pregnant**, breastfeeding, or undergoing fertility treatment;
- have cardiovascular disease, hypertension, a history of stroke, or arrhythmia;
- have chronic respiratory disease (asthma, COPD);
- have **diabetes**, thyroid disease, kidney or liver disease, or another metabolic disorder;
- have a current or past **eating disorder** (anorexia, binge eating, bulimia);
- have an unhealed **musculoskeletal injury**, disc herniation, or osteoporosis;
- have had **surgery** within the past 6 months;
- take medication affecting heart rate, blood pressure, blood glucose, electrolytes, or diuretics;
- are **under 18** or **over 65**;
- have any unexplained symptoms.

---

## 5. Stop exercising immediately if you experience

- chest pain or tightness, or pain radiating to the arm or jaw;
- dizziness, light-headedness, nausea, or near-fainting;
- breathlessness disproportionate to the effort;
- sharp joint or spinal pain, or a tearing/snapping sensation;
- irregular or very rapid heartbeat that does not settle with rest;
- blurred vision, ringing in the ears, or confusion.

Rest and **seek medical attention**. Mild post-workout soreness (DOMS) is normal; **sharp pain during a movement is not.**

## 6. Nutrition & allergies

6.1. **Food allergies can be fatal.** The AI is configured never to suggest items containing an allergen you declared, but **the system depends on the information you enter and on data supplied by merchants**. **You must confirm ingredients directly with the merchant before eating.**

6.2. SYNC **does not support** and will refuse requests involving: prolonged fasting, intake below safe calorie thresholds, unverified weight-loss drugs or teas, extreme detox regimes, or any dangerous rapid weight-loss method.

6.3. If you show signs of an **eating disorder** (weight preoccupation, compulsive calorie counting, purging, fear of eating), calorie tracking features may worsen the condition. Please consult a professional before use.

6.4. Diets for medical conditions (diabetes, kidney failure, gout, coeliac disease, and similar) **must be prescribed by a doctor or clinical dietitian**; do not substitute SYNC's suggestions.
