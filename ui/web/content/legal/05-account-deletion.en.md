## 1. Two ways to delete your account

### Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

**Profile → Account settings → Delete account** → read the §2 warnings carefully → **Confirm deletion** (two-step confirmation).

### Option 2 — By web / email (if you can no longer access the app)

Email **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** with:

- Subject: **"SYNC account deletion request"**
- Body: the email address registered to the account
- **Sent from that same registered email address** (so we can verify your identity)

We acknowledge within **72 hours** and delete immediately after verification.

| Action | Why |

## 3. What happens — timeline

### 3.1. IMMEDIATELY (within seconds)

| Action | Detail |
|---|---|
| Account status becomes **Deleted** | You can no longer sign in |
| **Identity data removed or replaced** | Email → internal anonymised token; Name → "Deleted User"; **phone number → deleted**; **avatar and cover image → deleted**; **password → wiped**; all email-verification and password-reset tokens → deleted |
| **All sessions revoked** | Every device signed out; refresh tokens invalidated and deleted |
| **Downgraded to Free & internal subscription expired** | The subscription record in SYNC is marked expired |
| **Community content anonymised** | Posts, comments, blogs, stories, blog comments → author name becomes **"Deleted user"**, avatar removed |

**Public content you posted is NOT deleted by default** — it is **anonymised**. Reason: other people's replies and discussions built on it would otherwise lose their context.
👉 **If you want your posts and images fully DELETED, delete each item BEFORE deleting your account**, or state "delete all my posted content" in your email to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will do it for you.

### 3.2. GRACE PERIOD — 30 DAYS

The account stays in a pending state for **30 days from your confirmation**.

- **Want it back?** Contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} **within 30 days**. We restore access along with your health data and training history.
  ⚠️ Items already hard-erased in step 3.1 (phone number, avatar, password) **cannot** be restored — you will need to set them again.
- During this period you cannot use the app.

**Why 30 days:** it protects you against accidental deletion and against someone who takes over your account and deletes it.

### 3.3. AFTER 30 DAYS — PERMANENT DELETION

The system automatically and **irreversibly** deletes:

| Data category | Result |
|---|---|
| Account profile | Deleted |
| **Biometric profile**: weight, height, body fat %, muscle mass, goals, BMR/TDEE, macros | **Deleted** |
| **Injuries, medications, allergies** | **Deleted** |
| **Biometric history over time** | **Deleted** |
| AI context profile (adherence, burnout risk, mood, and similar) | Deleted |
| Preferences, liked/disliked foods | Deleted |
| Roadmaps, schedules, workout logs, per-set logs, recovery profile | Deleted |
| Meal logs, daily nutrition summaries | Deleted |
| **AI long-term memory** (facts the AI remembered about you) | **Deleted** |
| AI conversations and caches | Deleted |
| Registered devices, push tokens | Deleted |
| Private images/videos in storage | Deleted |
| Follows/followers, likes, challenge participation | Deleted |
| Achievements, levels, items | Deleted |

## 4. Data we are REQUIRED to keep (and why)

We are transparent about what cannot be deleted on request, because the law requires retention:

| Data | Retention | Basis | How it is handled |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Invoices, transaction vouchers, accounting records** | **10 years** | Vietnamese accounting and tax law | Kept as financial vouchers, **separated from the user profile**; only the transaction reference and legally required fields remain |
| **Content report logs and outcomes** | 24 months | Community safety, appeals and repeat-offence handling | User identity anonymised |
| **Server security logs** (access traces, fraud prevention) | 90 days | Legitimate interest in system security | Auto-deleted by log lifecycle |
| **De-identified aggregate statistics** | Indefinite | No longer personal data | Cannot be traced back to you |
| **Public content others reshared or quoted** | Per that user's content lifecycle | Other users' rights | Already anonymised, no longer linked to you |
| Technical backups | Up to **35 days** from deletion | Disaster recovery | Rotated and overwritten; not queryable for ordinary purposes |

**None of the data above is used for marketing, user profiling, or AI training.**

## 6. Partial deletion (without deleting the whole account)

You do not have to delete everything. Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}:

| You want | Result |
|---|---|
| Delete **AI long-term memory** | The AI "forgets" everything it learned about you; account stays |
| Delete **all community content** you posted | Posts/comments/stories fully deleted; account stays |
| Delete **biometric history** | Account and roadmap kept; weight/body-fat time series deleted |
| Delete **order history** | Deletes everything not subject to mandatory retention under §4 |
| **Withdraw consent** for personalisation | The app keeps working; guidance becomes generic |

Processing time: **30 days** from identity verification.
