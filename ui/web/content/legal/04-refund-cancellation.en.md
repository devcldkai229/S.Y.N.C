## 1. Subscriptions purchased through Google Play (in the Android app)

### 1.1. Who handles refunds

When you buy Premium/Ultra **inside the Android app**, **Google is the merchant of record**. Refunds are **decided by Google under Google Play policy**; we cannot issue refunds for these transactions ourselves.

### 1.2. How to cancel renewal

1. Open **Google Play Store** → profile icon → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**
2. Select **SYNC** → **Cancel subscription**

Or in the SYNC app: **Profile → Subscription → Manage plan** (links to Google Play).

**Important:**
- Cancelling does **not** refund the current period — you **keep Premium benefits until the paid period ends**, then revert to Free.
- Cancel **at least 24 hours before** the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
- Uninstalling the app does **NOT** cancel the subscription. Deleting your SYNC account does **NOT** cancel it at Google — you must cancel separately in Google Play.

### 1.3. Requesting a refund from Google

- **Within 48 hours** of purchase: request directly at `play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory` — usually processed automatically.
- **After 48 hours:** submit a request to Google Play Support with your reason; Google reviews case by case.

### 1.4. What we do to help

Even though we are not the merchant of record, we will assist you:

- If a **technical fault on SYNC's side** prevents you from using benefits you paid for (e.g. purchase completed but the account was not upgraded), email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your **Google order ID (GPA.xxxx)**. We will: (a) fix it and restore your entitlement within **48 hours**; or (b) **extend your subscription** by the unusable period; or (c) provide written confirmation of the incident to support your refund request to Google.
- If we **discontinue the service** or materially reduce benefits during a paid period → see §5.

## 2. Subscriptions purchased on `{{WEBSITE}}`

For web purchases **we are the merchant of record** and handle refunds directly.

### 2.1. 7-day cancellation right

You receive a **100% refund** if you request it **within 7 days** of payment **and** you have not **materially used** the paid features in that period — specifically: no more than **10 Premium AI interactions**, no use of automatic ordering, and no unlocking of exclusive Premium content.

If you exceeded those thresholds we refund **pro rata for the remaining time** in the period, less any payment processor fee (if applicable, capped at 5%).

### 2.2. Cancelling renewal

In the app or on the web: **Profile → Subscription → Cancel renewal**. Benefits continue until the end of the paid period.

### 2.3. After 7 days

No refund for the current period, except in the cases in §5.

### 2.4. Annual plans

For annual plans cancelled after 7 days, you may request a refund **pro rata for full unused months**, less the difference between the annual and monthly rate for the months already used, and less payment processing fees.

### 2.5. Process & timelines

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your account email + transaction ID | — |
| We acknowledge receipt | **24 business hours** |
| Decision issued with reasons | **5 business days** |
| Funds returned to source account/wallet | **7–14 business days** after approval (depends on your bank/provider) |

## 3. Food orders

### 3.1. Cancelling an order

| Order status | Can cancel | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** confirmation | ✅ Freely in the app | **100%** |
| **Confirmed**, merchant not yet cooking | ✅ Yes | **100%** |
| **Preparing** | ⚠️ Subject to merchant approval | As agreed with the merchant; ingredient costs already incurred may be deducted |
| **Ready for pickup / Delivering** | ❌ Not unilaterally | Only where the merchant or courier is at fault |
| **Delivered / Completed** | ❌ | Only per §3.2 |

### 3.2. Refunds due to problems

You receive a **100% refund (including delivery fee)** where:

- the order **never arrived** within the committed window without valid reason;
- **wrong or missing items** compared with the confirmed order;
- food was **spoiled, contaminated, or contained a foreign object**, or was otherwise unsafe;
- the **merchant cancelled** after you paid;
- the dish contained an **allergen you explicitly noted on the order** while the menu carried no warning.

### 3.3. How to raise an order complaint

1. In the app: **Orders → select order → Report a problem**, or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
2. **Deadline: within 24 hours** of receiving the order (for food safety issues: as soon as possible).
3. **Evidence:** photos of the food/packaging and the order code. Without evidence we still review the case, but it takes longer.
4. We respond within **48 hours** and liaise with the Partner.

### 3.4. Refund method

| Original payment method | Refunded to |
|---|---|
| SYNC wallet | **SYNC wallet — within 24 hours** |
| VietQR / bank transfer (PayOS) | Source bank account — **3–7 business days**; or instantly to your SYNC wallet if you prefer |
| MoMo | Source MoMo wallet — **3–7 business days** |
| COD (not yet paid) | No refund arises |

### 3.5. AI-initiated orders

An order proposed by CYN AI **that you confirmed** counts as your own order; §3.1–3.4 apply unchanged.

**Exception:** if the system executed a transaction **without your confirmation step** (system fault), we refund **100% unconditionally** and cancel the order as soon as you report it. Please email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} — this is a serious incident and we will investigate.

### 3.6. Abuse

We may decline refund requests and restrict ordering where we detect systematic abuse (repeated false claims, mass cancellations after merchants have started cooking). We will state our reasons and you may appeal under §6.

## 5. Cases always eligible for a refund

Regardless of purchase channel, we refund the **unused portion pro rata** (or support your refund request to Google) where:

- we **discontinue the service** or the plan you are on;
- we **materially reduce plan benefits** during a period you already paid for;
- **duplicate charges** or incorrect amounts occur → **100%** of the incorrect amount;
- **unauthorised transactions** result from account compromise not caused by you (subject to verification);
- we suspend your account **without any breach on your part**.
