## 1. The spirit of this space

SYNC is for people pursuing a healthier life — at every body size, every level, every starting point.

Three principles:

1. **Safety first** — no content may put another person in physical or psychological danger.
2. **Honesty** — no false health claims, no faked results.
3. **Respect** — disagreement is fine; abuse is not.

## 2. Prohibited content

### 2.1. 🚫 Health-harmful content (strictest enforcement)

- Promoting **eating disorders**: "pro-ana"/"pro-mia" content, bone-showing, purging, "thinspiration".
- **Extreme fasting**, intake below safe calorie thresholds, "lose 10 kg in a week", total-fast detoxes.
- Selling or instructing use of **unverified weight-loss drugs**, dubious slimming teas, diuretics or laxatives for weight loss.
- **Anabolic steroids, growth hormone, doping**, SARMs, prescription drugs used off-label — including as "personal experience sharing".
- **Self-diagnosing or prescribing for others**, or advising someone to stop medication their doctor prescribed.
- Exercise technique **certain to cause injury** presented as instruction.
- Content encouraging **self-harm or suicide**.

### 2.2. 🚫 Medical misinformation

- Claims that food or exercise cures disease.
- False information about vaccines, medicines, or epidemics.
- Impersonating a doctor, pharmacist, dietitian, or certified trainer.
- Citing fabricated or distorted "scientific studies".

### 2.3. 🚫 Adult and violent content

- Pornography, sexualised nudity, sexual services.
- **Any content sexualising minors** → immediate action, permanent ban, and report to the authorities.
- Graphic violence, torture, animal abuse.
- Terrorist or violent extremist content.

> ℹ️ Progress photos in athletic wear or swimwear **are permitted** where the clear purpose is sharing progress, not sexual content.

### 2.4. 🚫 Harassment and hate

- Personal attacks, abuse, threats.
- **Body shaming** — demeaning anyone's appearance, weight, or physique in any form.
- Discrimination based on ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or region.
- Coordinated attacks or calls to pile on an account.
- **Doxxing** — publishing someone's personal information (address, phone, workplace).
- Posting photos or video of others without consent, especially in changing rooms or bathrooms.

### 2.5. 🚫 Spam, fraud, and manipulation

- Repetitive posting, junk content, irrelevant advertising.
- **Pyramid schemes, investment solicitation, financial fraud.**
- Selling accounts; buying or selling followers or likes.
- **Cheating community challenges**: inflated figures, multiple accounts, edited proof photos.
- Fake merchant reviews (self-promotion, attacking competitors, reviews traded for benefits).
- Links to malware or phishing.

### 2.6. 🚫 Intellectual property infringement

Posting someone else's photos, videos, articles, or training programmes without the right to do so.

### 2.7. 🚫 Abuse of CYN AI

- Attempting to make the AI generate content prohibited above.
- Prompt-injection attacks, extracting system instructions or other users' data.
- Using the AI to produce content impersonating a medical professional.

## 4. Moderation process & SLA

### 4.1. Severity tiers and response times

| Tier | Content type | First response | Final decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0 — Emergency** | Content sexualising minors; specific threats to life; content encouraging suicide or self-harm; terrorism | **≤ 1 hour** (content hidden on receipt) | **≤ 6 hours** |
| **P1 — High** | Health-harmful content (§2.1); sale of controlled substances or steroids; serious harassment, doxxing; private images posted without consent | **≤ 4 hours** | **≤ 24 hours** |
| **P2 — Medium** | Medical misinformation; body shaming; adult content; copyright infringement | **≤ 24 hours** | **≤ 72 hours** |
| **P3 — Low** | Spam, advertising, fake reviews, challenge cheating | **≤ 48 hours** | **≤ 7 days** |

*Times run from receipt; P0 and P1 apply on public holidays too.*

### 4.2. How cases are handled

1. **Intake** — every report is recorded with a tracked status (`Pending → Reviewing → Resolved / Rejected`).
2. **Temporary hiding** — P0 and P1 content is **hidden from the community immediately** while under review, to limit spread. Temporary hiding is **not** a finding of violation.
3. **Human review** — every removal or account action is **finally decided by a person**, never fully automated.
4. **Notice** — the poster is told which rule was breached and how to appeal.
5. **Record keeping** — case records are retained for 24 months for repeat-offence and appeal handling.

### 4.3. Enforcement ladder

| Offence | Minor (P2/P3) | Serious (P1) | Severe (P0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Content removed + warning | Removed + **7-day posting restriction** | **Immediate permanent ban** + report to authorities where required |
| 2nd | Removed + 3-day posting restriction | Removed + **30-day restriction** | — |
| 3rd | 14-day restriction | **Permanent ban** | — |
| 4th | Permanent ban | — | — |

**Fast-track permanent bans** bypassing the ladder apply to: content sexualising minors, specific threats to life, accounts created solely to spam or defraud, and accounts evading a ban by re-registering.

### 4.4. What we do beyond removal for self-harm content

Where content suggests a user is in psychological crisis, we **do not simply delete the post**. We send a supportive message with information about professional help resources and, where there is immediate risk, direct the person to **115** or the nearest medical facility. CYN AI is configured to respond the same way in conversation.

## 6. Appealing a moderation decision

You may appeal **any** content removal or account action.

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Submit | {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, subject **"Moderation appeal — [content/account ID]"**, within **30 days** of the notice |
| 2. Re-review | Conducted by **someone other than** the original decision-maker |
| 3. Response | **≤ 15 business days**, with reasons for upholding or overturning |
| 4. Restoration | If upheld in your favour: content restored, restrictions lifted, and the strike removed from your record |

You retain the right to complain to the competent state authority or bring proceedings under Vietnamese law.
